1. Introduction

TriagePaw ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered pet triage application.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: We collect your name and email via Google or Apple Authentication to manage your account and security.
  • Pet Health Data: Symptoms, triage history, and photos you provide are stored securely to provide your consultation history.
  • Device Identifiers (Anti-Fraud): We collect a unique hardware identifier (Device ID) solely to prevent abuse of our trial credit systems.
  • Camera & Photos: Accessed only for pet profiles and AI triage analysis. Images are stored securely in our cloud storage.
  • Microphone & Voice: Audio is processed in real-time for voice-to-text dictation and is not permanently stored or recorded.

3. AI Processing & Sub-Processors

We use industry-standard secure partners to provide our services. We do not sell your data to third-party advertisers.

  • Supabase: Our primary secure database and storage provider. Data is protected with Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): We use Anthropic's API for AI-powered triage analysis. Anthropic does not train its models on TriagePaw data. Data sent for analysis may be retained for up to 30 days for safety monitoring and then deleted.
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription entitlements. We do not store credit card details on our servers.
  • PostHog: Used for internal analytics to improve app performance and user experience.

4. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide our services. You can delete your account and all associated data (including pet profiles and history) directly from App Settings. This triggers a permanent cascade deletion from our systems.
