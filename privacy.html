3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Services
  • Generate AI-powered pet triage assessments based on your input
  • Maintain your pet's consultation history and health records
  • Manage your account, authentication, and credit balance
  • Send transactional emails (account verification, deletion confirmation)
  • Detect and prevent fraud and abuse
  • Analyse anonymised usage patterns to improve the app
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for targeted advertising, sell your data to third parties, or use your pet data to train AI models.

4. Camera, Microphone, and Photo Library Permissions

Camera

Camera access is requested when you take a photo of your pet for a profile picture or to submit as part of a triage consultation. Photos are uploaded directly to our secure cloud storage (Supabase Storage) and linked to your account. We do not access your camera at any other time.

Photo Library

Photo library access is requested when you choose to select an existing photo from your device for a pet profile or triage consultation. We access only the specific photo you select — we do not scan, browse, or access your full photo library.

Microphone

Microphone access is requested when you use the voice-to-text feature to dictate pet symptoms. Your voice recording is sent to a speech-to-text service for transcription and is not permanently stored. Only the resulting text transcription is saved as part of your consultation record.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide the Services. Specific retention periods:

  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account, then permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Pet profiles and consultation history: Retained until you delete your account or the individual record. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Consultation photos: Retained until you delete the consultation or your account. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Voice recordings: Not retained — processed in real-time for transcription only.
  • Device identifiers: Retained for fraud prevention purposes for up to 12 months after account deletion.
  • Purchase records: Retained for up to 7 years as required by Portuguese tax and commercial law.
  • Analytics data: Anonymised and retained indefinitely; cannot be linked back to individual users.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data. See Section 9 for how to delete your account.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing: Request that we restrict processing of your data under certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

9. Account Deletion and Data Removal

You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time through any of the following methods:

  • In-App (Recommended): Go to Account → Delete Account in the TriagePaw app. This permanently and immediately deletes your account and all associated data from our systems.
  • Website Form: Submit a request at https://triagepaw.com/deletion.html. You will receive a confirmation email; your account will be processed for deletion within 30 days.
  • Email: Contact us at contact@triagepaw.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

Upon deletion, the following data is permanently removed: your account profile, pet profiles, consultation history, uploaded photos, and credit balance. Anonymised analytics data that cannot be linked to you may be retained. Certain financial records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by law.

10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Row-Level Security (RLS) in our database — each user can only access their own data
  • Encrypted data transmission over HTTPS/TLS
  • Secure cloud storage with access controls for uploaded photos
  • Authentication via industry-standard providers (Supabase Auth, Google, Apple)
  • Regular security reviews of our infrastructure

While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected users and the relevant supervisory authority as required by GDPR.
